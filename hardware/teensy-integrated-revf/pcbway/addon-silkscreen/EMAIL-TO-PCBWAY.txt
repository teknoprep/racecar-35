Hi Kelly,

Two things for the Rev F package you already have.

1) VIA IN SMD PAD - please use option A, and only for 2 vias
   Please use option A (via in pad: filled with resin, then capped and plated over).
   Only 2 vias on this board sit inside SMD paste apertures: 0.6 mm land / 0.3 mm
   drill, front side, in the pads of R35 and R34 (0805). Those are the only ones
   that need the fill.
   Please do NOT quote option B (all vias filled): the board has 125 through vias
   in total and the other 123 are ordinary mask-tented signal vias with no paste
   opening over them, so filling them adds cost with no benefit.
   No paste-aperture reduction is needed - the capped vias end up flush with the pad.
   (If your DFM list also includes the 12 x 0.3 mm holes inside the ESP32-S3
   module's 3.9 mm centre ground pad, those carry no paste aperture at all, so no
   solder can flow into them. They need no treatment, but including them in the same
   fill operation is fine.)
   -> Please confirm the price for the via-in-pad fill on 2 vias so I can approve it.
