Parts we are changing:

1. J8 (item 37) - Change: 901-143 -> 901-143-6RFX (Amphenol RF)

2. D23 (item 7) - Change: SS56 -> an SMB (DO-214AA) 5 A / 60 V Schottky, e.g. BORN SS56 SMB (LCSC C2687867) or Diotec SK56
Why: You are right: the bare SS56 is normally supplied as SMA (DO-214AC) and will not fit our DO-214AA (SMB) land pattern. Please supply the SMB-packaged part only - not the DO-214AC one.

3. U1 (item 27) - Change: Re-add 2x SSW-124-01-G-S (1x24, 2.54 mm, THT female socket)
Why: This line was dropped from the revised quotation. The Teensy 4.1 is customer-supplied - we buy it with pins and plug it in. You source and fit the two sockets only.

4. H1-H4 (item 75), TP1-TP5 (item 76) - Change: Mark "no part - do not source, do not place"
Why: These are not components, which is why your system cannot find a part number or brand. H1-H4 are 3.2 mm mounting holes in the PCB; TP are 2.0 mm plated through-hole test pads (bare copper). Nothing to buy, nothing to place, no assembly cost.

5. D24 (item 3) - Change: Accept your BZX84-C15,215
Why: Correct - that is Nexperia's orderable MPN (same device).
